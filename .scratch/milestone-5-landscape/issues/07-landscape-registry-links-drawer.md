## What to build

The landscape as the fifth problem of the registry (logo `Ls`, provisional defaults until ticket 14, no presets yet), in the problem menu through the restart notice; its options (function, representation, bits per variable) and its parameters (operator with α, mutation with σ, ε) carried by share links and validated in the drawer's words, including a real-only operator on a binary-coded problem and the reverse; the drawer's landscape section as the mock decided (function, representation, bits per variable, operator and mutation settings of the chosen representation only, ε), staged and applied with "Restart and apply"; switching representation restarts and resets the crossover and mutation settings to the new representation's defaults, and the restart notice lists exactly what is reset; hand-edited populations typed as number pairs or bit strings; the brand line.

## Done when

- Generated landscape configurations (functions, representations, bits, operators with α and σ, hand-edited populations) round-trip through links with run equality; every bad-option case gives a readable message and applies nothing, the address is cleared; the drawer's validation names the field; the restart notice for a representation switch is asserted word for word.
- Playwright: switch to Landscape through the menu, change each option through the drawer and compare with the engine, open a link in a fresh page; no control without a name with the drawer open; the other problems' menus, drawers, defaults and links unchanged.
