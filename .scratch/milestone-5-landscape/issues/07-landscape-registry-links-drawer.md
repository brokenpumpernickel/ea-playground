## What to build

The landscape as the fifth problem of the registry (logo `Ls`, provisional defaults until ticket 14, no presets yet), in the problem menu through the restart notice; its options (function, representation, bits per variable) and its parameters (operator with α, mutation with σ, ε) carried by share links and validated in the drawer's words, including a real-only operator on a binary-coded problem and the reverse; the drawer's landscape section as the mock decided (function, representation, bits per variable, operator and mutation settings of the chosen representation only, ε), staged and applied with "Restart and apply"; switching representation restarts and resets the crossover and mutation settings to the new representation's defaults, and the restart notice lists exactly what is reset; hand-edited populations typed as number pairs or bit strings; the brand line.

## Answer
The landscape is the fifth problem of the registry and works end to end in the menu, the drawer and share links, in all three representations.

- **Engine additions this needed**: `StopConfig.targetEpsilon` (the criterion "target reached" when the best so far is at least the problem's known optimum minus ε; `stopReasons` takes the optimum as a third argument and refuses ε ≤ 0 or an unknown optimum in words), which replaces the `landscapeTargetFitness` helper of ticket 06 so that ε is carried exactly as typed; `landscapeDefaults(options)` (provisional numbers, replaced by measured ones in ticket 14: population 20, tournament 3, BLX-α 0.9 with α 0.5 and Gaussian per gene 0.1 with σ 0.05 for real genes; one-point 0.8 and bit-flip per gene 0.02 for the binary-coded ones; one elite; stop within the function's default ε, stagnation 40).
- **Registry** (`problems.ts`): entry `landscape`, name Landscape, logo `Ls`, brand line "Single peak · maximise f · optimum 1 · real-valued" (or "… · Gray-coded, 8 bits each"), options validated per field in words, `defaultsFor(options)` so a fresh run opens with the operators of its representation. The problem menu, the restart notice and the switch use it.
- **Links** (`run-config.ts`): new optional keys `crossover.alpha`, `mutation.operator` and `mutation.sigma`, `stop.targetEpsilon`; the operator list gained `blx-alpha` and `arithmetic`. Everything the drawer would refuse is refused with its words and nothing is used: unknown function or representation, bits out of range, a real-only crossover type for bits (and for any symbol problem), α negative or on another operator, σ not above 0 or on a symbol problem, a mutation operator of the wrong representation, ε not above 0, a target fitness on the landscape, hand-edited populations that are not numbers, have the wrong count or leave the bounds, or bit strings of the wrong length.
- **Drawer** (`drawer-model.ts`, `Drawer.svelte`): Function, Representation and Bits per variable (binary-coded only) in the setup; the crossover list of the representation (BLX-α and Arithmetic only for real genes; Two-point labelled "needs 3 genes" there and refused in words); α while BLX-α is chosen, σ for real genes, "Within ε of the optimum" in place of the target fitness; hand-edited populations typed one per line as numbers separated by spaces or commas (rows show the decoded point and the table cells) or as bit strings. α, σ and ε are operator parameters (pending until the next generation boundary); function, representation and bits need a restart. Switching between real and bit genes resets the crossover type and the mutation to the new representation's defaults (the on/off switches and pc are kept; plain ↔ Gray keeps everything), and the drawer's help line and the restart notice list exactly what was reset, e.g. "Reset to the new representation’s defaults: crossover type (BLX-α → One-point), mutation (Gaussian, per gene, pm 0.1, σ = 0.05 → bit flip, per gene, pm 0.02)."; operator edits are not applied to the running representation while the form holds another one.
- **Display**: real chromosomes are shown to three decimals (`displayOf`); the other problems' views are untouched. The panels specific to the landscape (table cells, operator cards, landscape view, spread panel) are tickets 08 to 13.
- **Tests**: `problems-landscape.test.ts` (9), `run-config-landscape.test.ts` (35: round trips with run equality for all 12 function × representation pairs, every operator with α/σ, hand-edited populations of both kinds, ε, and each bad-link case), `drawer-landscape.test.ts` (30: draft, per-field validation, population parsing, representation switch and the notice word for word, what changes and when) and `e2e/landscape-drawer.spec.ts` (9: menu and restart notice, each option through the drawer compared with the engine's run, the Gray switch notice, α/σ/ε and their errors, hand-edited pairs, links in a fresh page for each representation, a refused link, every control named for two representations). Existing expectations that listed four problems or four operators were updated; nothing else changed. Mutation-checked (real-only refusal, representation reset, real population splitting, ε in the parameters, unused α, target-on-landscape refusal): each fails tests.
- **Found on the way**: the browser's `Math.log` and `Math.cos` differ from Node's in the last bits, so the Gaussian step moves a fitness by about 1e-15 between the page and the engine in the tests; the e2e checks compare the page with the engine to nine decimals, and ADR 0006 now says so (a run is reproducible by seed in the same implementation).

Golden runs unchanged; `npm run check` clean; 90 unit-test files / 1689 tests pass; the whole end-to-end suite (440 tests) passes after two expectations that listed four problems were updated.
