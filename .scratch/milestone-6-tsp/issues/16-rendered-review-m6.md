### The eight acceptance criteria and their evidence

1. **Existing problems untouched.** The golden runs (`src/engine/golden.test.ts`) reproduce populations, events and original metrics exactly after every engine change of the milestone; every milestone 1–5 unit and end-to-end test passes (the full suites above); the other problems' menus, links, presets (`string-link.spec.ts` loops over all of them) and strips are asserted unchanged where a TSP test touches them (`tsp-plots.spec.ts` "the other problems keep their strip", `tsp-presets.spec.ts`, the menu lists in `problem.spec.ts`, `trap-link.spec.ts`, `landscape-drawer.spec.ts`). Two older tests that counted on the five-problem menu or on a count of presets were updated for the sixth problem.
2. **Instances and distances.** `tsp.test.ts` (41 tests): EUC, EUC_2D, ATT and GEO on hand-worked pairs (the GEO constants pinned with equator pairs); every stored optimal tour recomputes to the published optimum (ulysses16 6,859, ulysses22 7,013, att48 10,628, eil51 426, berlin52 7,542, burma14 by Held–Karp 3,323); Held–Karp against brute force for every instance of up to 8 cities and for seeded random ones; the circle's optimum equals its perimeter; a seeded random instance is the same on every run; the nearest-neighbour tour is hand-worked on a small instance (and its lengths 8,980 for berlin52 and 21.0 % for att48 are used by the plots and the presets); fitness = −length; every individual of every run of every instance, crossover and mutation is a permutation (`tsp-run.test.ts`).
3. **Operators.** `crossover-permutation.test.ts` (19), `mutation-permutation.test.ts` (20), `permutation.test.ts` (24): OX, PMX and CX hand-worked on the textbook parents of Eiben and Smith, with the events; swap, inversion and insertion change exactly the positions their events state; per-individual probability, bypasses, probabilities 0 and 1; two equal cuts, cuts at the ends and n = 3; the symbol operators refused for a permutation problem and the permutation operators for the others, with a readable message in the drawer (`drawer-tsp.test.ts`) and in links (`run-config-tsp.test.ts`); no two individuals share an array.
4. **Reproducibility and agreement.** `tsp-run.test.ts`: a seeded run identical by micro-step, stage, generation, run-until and every playback tier, for every instance; replaying a generation's events reproduces the population; roulette with negative fitness through a visible weight chain; every event's numbers agree with the views: the operator cards, the narration, the map and the plots are all compared with the events (`tsp-operators`, `tsp-map`, `tsp-plots`, `tsp-table` and `classroom-m6` specs).
5. **Views agree with the engine.** `tsp-map.spec.ts`: every drawn edge, its count and width, the best, selected and optimal tours, the stage marks at every micro-step of two generations for OX, PMX and CX, the figures beside the map, the same individual in the table and on the map, GEO, EUC_2D and ATT cities where their coordinates say; `tsp-plots.spec.ts`: plotted and printed values equal the metrics at every recorded generation, the edge-agreement bars, counts and heights; `tsp-table.spec.ts`: every row's tour, length and gap; `tsp-operators.spec.ts`: every pair and offspring of two generations for all six operators on 5, 16 and 52 cities.
6. **Links and presets.** `run-config-tsp.test.ts`: every instance kind, every operator pair, switched-off operators, elites, truncation, the stops and a hand-edited population (with a rotation, a reversal and commas) round-trip as the same run (populations, events, metrics), and every bad-option case is said in words and applies nothing; `string-link.spec.ts` and `tsp-presets.spec.ts`: the link of each of the 37 presets of all six problems opens the same run in a fresh page; `presets-tsp.test.ts` and the statistics files: each TSP text's every number is asserted on its seed and over seeds 1–30.
7. **Accessibility.** `tsp-a11y.spec.ts` (54) and the audit list in ticket 15: the colour-coded meanings with their non-colour carriers, a full generation by keyboard alone with the map and a selection from it, no unnamed control with the menu, drawer, map and selection open, reduced motion, projector mode and not, both sizes, ten cases (5 to 52 cities, 12 and 60 individuals, every instance kind, tours all the same and every other one reversed), the top bar with the longest preset and instance names, the ribbon, every stage pill and a three-digit generation counter.
