## What to build

The selection view with sharing gets the columns "niche count" and "shared" after the weight chain's last column and says what they are; the tournament view shows the shared weights it compared; the selected individual gets a σ-radius ring on the heatmap (and a note); with crowding the selection view says "every individual once, in random order"; the replacement view shows the duels (pair, parents, children, the matching and its distances, each duel's result with the discarded mark on the loser); the narration and the sentences say the same.

## Comments

## Answer

**Built:** the views for niching. **Selection with fitness sharing** (`RouletteView`, `TournamentView`, `selection-models.ts`): the roulette weight table gets the columns "Niche count" and "Shared" after the weight chain, the formula line says "… → ÷ niche count (fitness sharing, σ = 2, α = 1) → shared weight → probability", and the wheel's slices and intervals are the shared probabilities; the tournament view says it compares shared weights, shows a table of every individual's fitness, niche count and shared weight, and writes each contestant as "shared x (fitness y)". **Deterministic crowding's selection** (new `CrowdingSelectionView`): one card per draw ("n: not drawn" until its micro-step, then r and the individual it picked), the pool filling in order, and the pairing note "every individual is a parent exactly once, in random order". **Its replacement** (`ReplacementView`, `outcome-models.ts`): the duels replace the Offspring column; a table by pair with a pair row ("parents a and b · child 1 ↔ A, child 2 ↔ B, total distance … straight, … crossed: the smaller is chosen"), and a row for each child (the child, the parent it duelled, their distance, the two fitnesses, "child wins ✓ · #parent ✕" or "#parent stays ✓ · On ✕", the winner's text carrying the mark as well as the colour); the next population marks the places that kept their old individual ("kept #id"); the explanation says how many children won. **The niche ring** (`Heatmap`, `LandscapeView`): with sharing and an individual selected, a dashed ring of radius σ lies on the heatmap centred on that individual, as an ellipse (the two axes may have different scales), clipped to the plot, with "σ = 2" written above it and a sentence under the view; none without a selection, under crowding or with no niching; it follows into the focus view. The narration (`describe.ts`) and the sentences say the same.

**Layout:** the first duels table overlapped the next-population column and the projector font rules overrode mine; the table is now fixed-layout, headings and pair rows in their own rows, with its own projector sizes. A weights table with the extra columns is narrower in type (the wheel is smaller beside it). In projector mode at 1920 and 12 individuals the roulette's table shows one or two rows before scrolling, as the plain roulette view already did (the draws and the mating pool take the room); I did not redesign that.

**Tests:** unit (`niching-views.test.ts`, 19): the roulette and tournament models with hand-worked niche counts (1 + 2 × 0.95 …), the crowding selection model's draws and pool state, the pairing notes, the duel rows and their winners, the next population's "kept" sources. End-to-end (`e2e/niching-views.spec.ts`, 28): the sharing table equal to the engine's counts and shared weights for every individual; no niche columns without niching; the tournament table; crowding's draws, each micro-step showing the engine's r and individual; the duels equal to the engine's (matching, distances, winners); the ring's centre and radii from the heatmap's layout, clipped, with its label and sentence, gone without a selection or under crowding or none, in the focus view too; and, for five setups (sharing with roulette, with tournaments, crowding at 12, 40 and an odd 13 individuals) at 1920×1080 and 1366×768, normal and projector, **every micro-step** of two generations: views inside the stage body, no sideways scrolling inside the weights, duels or tables, the duels not overlapping the next population, the narration whole and the page not scrolling. The first version of that check sampled every fifth step and missed the replacement stage (one micro-step) in four of five runs; a mutation (the duel table at 160% width) went unnoticed until it checked every step, and is now caught. 2,386 unit tests, 1,067 end-to-end tests (55 skipped without their switches) and `npm run check` pass.
